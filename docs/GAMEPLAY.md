# Gameplay Overview

## About

Miner's Haven is a collaborative Roblox island simulator focused on exploration, mining, resource collection, and progression.

Players explore different islands, gather valuable resources, discover hidden artifacts, and improve their equipment as they progress.

## Core Gameplay Loop

**Explore → Mine → Collect → Sell → Upgrade → Unlock**

Each stage contributes to player progression and encourages exploration of new islands.

## Main Gameplay Features

### Mining & Resource Collection
Players mine resource deposits across islands to collect valuable materials.

### Artifact Discovery
Hidden artifacts can be discovered while mining. Each artifact belongs to a rarity tier, making some discoveries more valuable and harder to obtain.

### Artifact Index
The Artifact Index tracks discovered artifacts and rewards players with permanent progression bonuses.

### Inventory & Selling
Collected resources can be stored and sold to earn in-game currency.

### Pickaxe Progression
Players can upgrade their pickaxes to improve their mining capabilities and progress further.

### Island Progression
New islands become available as players meet progression requirements. Previously unlocked islands remain accessible.

### Minions
Minions are planned as companions that assist players with mining.

## Development Status

Miner's Haven is actively being developed. Core gameplay and progression systems have been implemented, while additional environments and features are still being worked on.
